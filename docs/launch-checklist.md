# Launch checklist

Before you share your author site with the world, check each item below.

- [ ] Repository is created and set to **Public**
- [ ] GitHub Pages is enabled in Settings → Pages
- [ ] Source branch is set (usually `main`) and the site builds with a green checkmark
- [ ] A theme is chosen, or your own HTML files are uploaded
- [ ] Home page (`index.html`) exists and loads
- [ ] Your name, author photo, and bio are on the site
- [ ] Your books are listed with covers and descriptions
- [ ] Contact information is correct and working
- [ ] All internal links open the right pages
- [ ] The site looks good on a phone and on a computer
- [ ] Spelling and typos have been checked
- [ ] The site link (`https://yourusername.github.io/`) opens in a private or incognito window
- [ ] You have saved the repository link somewhere safe

When every box is checked, your site is ready to share. Add the link to your email signature, your book's back matter, and your social media profiles.
