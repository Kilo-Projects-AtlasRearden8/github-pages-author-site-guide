# 5. Publish your site

Your site is published as soon as GitHub finishes building it.

## Check that it is live

1. Open your repository settings, then click **Pages**.
2. Click the site link (for example, `https://yourusername.github.io/`).
3. Your home page should appear.

## Share it

- Add the link to your email signature
- Add it to your book's back matter
- Share it on social media

## Keep it current

- Add new books as you publish them
- Update your bio when you have news
- Check the site once a month to make sure links still work

Congratulations — you are now a published author with a website!
