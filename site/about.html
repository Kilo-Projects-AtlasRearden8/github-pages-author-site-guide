About

Nurse turned AI author

I'm Dylan Asdale. I worked as a registered nurse, then taught myself AI and started writing guides that explain AI "like a friend would" — plain English, step by step, never condescending.

I'm the founder of Asdale Dynamics, LLC, an Indiana LLC formed in October 2026 focused on beginner-friendly AI education, books and guides, and practical tools.

My book, The Massive AI Manual, walks beginners through AI tools, agents, local AI, and free AI coding — no prior experience required.

What Asdale Dynamics, LLC publishes

  • Beginner AI manuals and guides
  • Open-source apps and templates (prompt tools, local-LLM utilities, MCP servers)
  • Author and small-business tools (KDP, marketing, bookkeeping)
  • Accessible life-admin and low-energy productivity helpers
  • Healthcare-worker AI aids (education only, never medical advice)
  • Gaming and chess tools
